Privacy Policy — NegatTech AI

Last updated: September 2026

1. Information We Collect

We collect the following information to provide our service:

  • Business information you provide (name, location, hours, products)
  • TikTok account information obtained through TikTok OAuth (username, display name, user ID)
  • TikTok comments and messages that are sent to your business account
  • Your account email and encrypted password

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information solely to:

  • Generate AI-powered replies to your customers' TikTok comments and messages
  • Display your business dashboard and analytics
  • Authenticate your TikTok account to send replies on your behalf

3. TikTok Data

We access TikTok data only through TikTok's official API with your explicit authorization. We access only the scopes you approve. We do not access your personal TikTok content beyond what is needed for the service. TikTok access tokens are stored securely and encrypted.

4. Data Storage

Your data is stored locally on your own computer using SQLite. We do not store your data on external cloud servers unless you explicitly configure this.

5. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or share your personal information with third parties. We do not share your TikTok data with any external services except TikTok's own API when sending replies on your behalf.

6. Data Retention

You can delete your data at any time by deleting your account. We retain data only as long as needed to provide the service.

7. Security

Passwords are encrypted using bcrypt. TikTok tokens are stored securely. We use HTTPS for all communications.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or delete your personal data at any time. You can revoke TikTok access at any time through TikTok's settings or through our app's TikTok page.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions, contact us through the app.
